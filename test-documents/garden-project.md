# Garden Project Notes

A small community garden planned for the empty lot behind the library.

## Budget

The total budget for the garden project is 4,500 euros. The plants take 1,200 euros and the greenhouse takes 2,100 euros.
The remaining 1,200 euros covers tools and soil.

## Schedule

The soil is delivered in the first week of April. The greenhouse is delivered in the second week of May and takes two days to build.
Planting starts on May 20 and the first harvest is expected in late July.

## Team

Marta leads the project and keeps the budget. Joao is in charge of the greenhouse. Priya organises the volunteers, and about twelve
people have signed up so far. Volunteers meet every Saturday at 9:00.

## Rules

Plots are 3 by 4 metres. Each household may rent one plot. Pesticides are not allowed; use compost instead.
