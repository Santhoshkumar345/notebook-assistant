# Garden Project Notes (revised)

A small community garden planned for the empty lot behind the library.

## Budget

The total budget for the garden project is now 5,000 euros. The plants take 1,200 euros and the greenhouse takes 2,400 euros.
The remaining 1,400 euros covers tools and soil.

## Schedule

The soil is delivered in the first week of April. The greenhouse is delivered in the third week of May and takes two days to build.
Planting starts on June 1 and the first harvest is expected in August.
