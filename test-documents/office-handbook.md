# Office Handbook

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours people may start and finish when it suits them, as long as they work 8 hours a day.

## Remote work

Staff may work from home up to 3 days a week. The two office days are agreed with the team lead. Remote work from another country
needs written approval from the team lead and is limited to 20 days a year.

## Expenses

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 50 euros per day. Hotels are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night. Receipts must be
uploaded within 14 days, otherwise the expense is not paid.

## Vacation

Every employee has 25 vacation days a year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year, and they expire on March 31.
Requests of more than 10 days in a row need approval two months in advance.

## Security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Lost laptops must be reported to IT on extension 4411 within one hour.
Never forward company documents to a personal email address.

## Contacts

For payroll questions write to payroll@example.test. For anything about equipment call IT on extension 4411.
