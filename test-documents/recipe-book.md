# Recipe Book

## Shakshuka

Serves 4. Cook 1 diced onion and 1 red pepper in olive oil for 8 minutes. Add 3 cloves of garlic, 2 teaspoons of cumin and
1 teaspoon of paprika. Pour in one 800 g can of crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes. Make 6 small wells in the sauce, crack an
egg into each, cover and cook for 6 minutes until the whites are set.

## Pancakes

Makes about 8. Whisk 200 g of flour, 2 tablespoons of sugar and 2 teaspoons of baking powder. Mix in 300 ml of milk, 1 egg and
30 g of melted butter. Rest the batter for 10 minutes. Cook a ladle of batter per pancake on a medium pan, about 2 minutes on
each side.

## Lentil soup

Serves 6. Fry 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 sticks of celery for 10 minutes. Add 250 g of red lentils, 1.2 litres of vegetable stock
and 1 teaspoon of turmeric. Simmer for 25 minutes, then blend half of the soup and stir it back in. Finish with the juice of
half a lemon.
